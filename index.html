The Highspire promise

Never the same game twice.

We're a small, independent studio. We'd rather make one surprising game than ten safe copies, and we treat every player the way we'd want our own family treated.

A new world every time

Each Highspire game gets its own idea, art style and feel. If it's been done before, we don't make it.

Fair play, always

No loot boxes, no pay-to-win, no countdown pressure. Anything you can buy is cosmetic and shown up front.

Made for everyone

Big clear text, colour-blind-friendly palettes and 20 languages, so grandkids and grandparents can play side by side.

Game 01 · Out now in early access

Kinhollow

A village lost in fog, rebuilt by your whole family, one small puzzle at a time. Each puzzle you solve brings timber, stone and glass home. Together you restore houses, a water mill, an orchard and a lantern tower that glows across the valley.

Play right here

Light the lantern.

A tiny Fog Garden, playable on this page. Guide the lantern from the bottom-left to the golden lamp post. A safe path is hidden in the fog; step into a pond and you lose a light.

  1. Tap a glowing square next to your lantern to step there.
  2. Numbers tell you how many ponds touch that square.
  3. You have 3 lights. Reach the lamp post to win.
